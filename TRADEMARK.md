These remain the property of Kevin T. Coughlin and may not be used to name, brand, or market a
fork or derived site or product without written permission.

## What you can do freely

- Fork, modify, build, and redistribute the code under its license —
  **under a different name, icon, and domain**
- Build and run the site from source for yourself, unchanged, including its name and icon
- Use the name "Juniper Leaves" nominatively: to refer to this project or shop in articles,
  reviews, compatibility notes ("based on Juniper Leaves"), and attribution

## Why

This is the standard FOSS arrangement (used by Firefox, VLC, and many others): the code is free,
the identity is not. It protects customers from confusing or misleading copies of the storefront
without restricting any freedom the license grants.
